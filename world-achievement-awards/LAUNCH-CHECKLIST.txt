LIMITS BEFORE A FULL PUBLIC LAUNCH
- Browser checks are not a cross-browser/device-lab or screen-reader certification; Core Web Vitals have not been measured.
- Automated storage tests use an in-memory R2 substitute. Live contact persistence and retrieval in the owner inbox were verified after deployment. A labelled synthetic QA contact record remains in the inbox. Live nomination attachments have not been exercised.
- R2 rate and duplicate checks prevent sequential abuse/retries, but are not atomic under simultaneous requests. A public high-traffic launch needs an atomic limiter/idempotency store and anti-bot controls.
- File checks inspect size and signature, not malware. Files are private, downloaded as attachments, and never executed by the app. Consider scanning before expanding upload access.
- Content editing uses a single published collection; concurrent editors should not publish simultaneously.
- Video demo cards are labelled samples, not official footage. Real YouTube players activate when approved video links are published.
